      Caed Nua is not yours yet

      This save has no stronghold to manage. The keep becomes yours during Never Far From the Queen, in Act I, and nothing here can be edited until it does.

      Granting it from the editor is deliberately not offered: taking the keep moves the party, advances a quest and spawns the steward, and flipping the ownership flag on its own would leave a stronghold with none of that.
